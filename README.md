**Bookini** is a hotel booking management application designed to simplify reservations, streamline guest check-ins, and optimize room availability.  
Whether you're managing a boutique inn or a large hotel chain, Bookini helps you stay on top of your bookings with ease.  

---

### ✨ Features

- 📅 **Reservation Management** – Create, modify, and cancel bookings with ease  
- 🛎️ **Guest Check-in & Check-out** – Track arrivals and departures in real-time  
- 🛏️ **Room Availability Tracking** – Prevent double-bookings and maximize occupancy  
- 📊 **Dashboard & Reports** – Get insights on bookings, revenue, and occupancy trends  
- 🔒 **Secure & Reliable** – Built with security and data integrity in mind
